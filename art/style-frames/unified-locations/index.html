Three locations, one game

Near-monochrome scenery changes by location. Warm-coloured people and animals, the blue UFO, mint abduction effects and coral military warnings keep the same visual roles throughout.

Sage Farmland

Open paddocks, low barn roofs and crop-circle traces; the whole environment shares a muted sage-grey family.

Slate Suburbia

Sleepy residential streets and front gardens, all rendered in subdued slate-blue while the shared warm target palette remains intact.

Mauve Desert Roadside

Broad desert ground, angular rock outcrops, cacti and a roadside canopy in muted mauve; the same coloured cast and effects maintain the game's identity.
