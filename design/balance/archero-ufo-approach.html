Archero progression.
An endless UFO run.

Play immediately, choose temporary abilities during the attempt, then use earned credits to improve the next starting ship. Keep one optional Hangar screen and let equipment unlocks join future runs automatically.

The owner selected Archero as the primary reference. This plan adopts its two-layer progression, adapting it to the UFO runner. 19,360 runs are recorded across the current comparison, confirmation, sensitivity, repeated-account and unlock follow-up datasets. The fresh mixed-build median is 6.16 active minutes; the fully developed account median is 9.22.

The official Archero listing describes random skills and equipment that increases character stats. Our particular values, six-module boundary, endless director, eligibility rules and rewards are proposed UFO design decisions, not claims about Archero’s exact rules or economy.

The approach to build

  1. Start. One starting laser and one beam, plus purchased Hangar stat bonuses. No mandatory blueprint preparation. Lasers continue firing while the UFO moves.
  2. Upgrade during the run. Earn XP, pause, see three distinct eligible cards and choose one. Start with the 16 + 3 × current level XP threshold. Preserve surplus XP. Install at most six modules and improve each through three separate picks. Beam Splitter still gives +1 beam per pick: 1 → 2 → 3 → 4.
  3. Build towards combinations. First two completed rank-III recipes evolve automatically. Research sustains choices beyond completed modules. Two free rerolls per attempt; no ads on level-up cards. Visible rarity remains 60/35/5 for new blueprint/research quality draws, with unavailable tiers renormalized; owned ranks draw from Common while keeping their original rarity label.
  4. End, bank and retry. Death ends the attempt unless an eligible optional revive completes. Bank Research Credits and achievement progress. Retry immediately or spend credits in the Hangar. Run levels, modules, temporary research and evolutions reset.

Use ninety-second sectors as distance/record goals within the endless run. Sectors do not open shops, refill the ship, reset difficulty or end the attempt. The current director escalates from active run time, shared by all accounts and independent of their purchases, equipment or ad choices.

Each track costs 80, 140, 240, 400, 640 credits for ranks I–V: twenty purchases and 6,000 credits to cap the Hangar. These gains add to fixed starting templates. Permanent upgrades never add lasers, beams, reflection chance, invulnerability or faster shield regeneration; those build changes remain earned during each run. Run modifiers then act on the improved templates.

Keep run XP and Research Credits visibly separate. Runs lasting at least thirty active seconds award floor(seconds/8) + floor(score/5) + 2×vehicle takedowns + 5×boss kills credits. Each takedown is counted once. Death or voluntary ending banks earned rewards under the same rules. A spend screen is optional, and there is no entry currency.

Progress without ads; optional rewards accelerate it

For regular Hybrid accounts, the median first purchase is attempt 1.0. Permanent power caps at attempt 22.5 without ads, 15.0 with the +50% credit reward always completed, and 12.0 with both reward types always completed. The double-credit comparison caps at 9.0 with both rewards, which consumes this finite progression goal sooner.

  • Revive: at most one after ninety active seconds; restore half maximum hull, zero shield and three seconds of invulnerability. Clear ordinary active threats, retain bosses, XP, build and run time.
  • Credit reward: one completed optional placement grants floor(base credits/2) extra. No extra XP or achievement credit. Hide it once remaining permanent ranks are already affordable.
  • Cosmetic packs: a later purchase option. The first playable uses local ad/billing test doubles and needs no online service.

Declining an offer, unavailable inventory, failure or incomplete ads permit ordinary results/restart. Rewards settle once. The +50% amount is an economy candidate; this study does not measure whether players want that reward or forecast ad revenue.

What the comparisons changed

  • Use the steadier XP curve: the faster curve produces more picks and longer attempts; the slowest curve leaves many builds without a completed evolution.
  • Keep six installed modules. Allowing all sixteen families to be installed spreads upgrades out and reduces evolution completion for several preset policies. It remains a design choice, not a direct copy of Archero.
  • Use capped +25% weapon damage as the starting permanent-power candidate. The larger +50% profile extends runs further and creates a stronger progression dependency; it is available in the comparison data for review.
  • Use automatic unlocked-card eligibility, with prerequisites that make vehicle/conditional technologies usable. Keep the Hangar focused on four stat tracks.
  • Test +50% credits rather than doubling to preserve more of the first-playable progression goal.

Remaining risks and next evidence

Collector and other specialist rewards still require tuning, and new card options can weaken individual random drafts. The bots do not plan recipes optimally. Do not interpret preset survival rankings as settled human build balance. Check upgrade clarity, meaningful choices, avoidable deaths, revive value and voluntary replay with repeated hands-on attempts.

The encounter model still approximates spatial movement, dodging, capture retention and laser targeting. Thrusters affect both dodging and encounter/lifetime proxies; actual lateral movement may behave differently. Laser/drone damage is pooled; geometry, visual readability and input latency are unmeasured. Bosses may leave after ninety seconds, so survival does not imply killing them. Vehicle capture score remains an assumed five. There is no native gameplay, human fun, learning, churn, retention, eCPM, ad-fill, purchase-conversion or revenue measurement.

The Hangar and six unlocks are a finite first-playable goal. Continued play can pursue score, distance and builds; this is not a content plan for months of live operation. Keep the chosen Archero progression model stable while using human/device evidence to revise numbers.

Implementation order

  1. Portable UFO run: movement, automatic lasers, capture, military attacks, death/restart and paused three-card level-ups with exact gains.
  2. Level curve and active-time director; earned evolutions and visible next-sector/record goals. No forced death time or power-matching enemy scaling.
  3. One Hangar screen: Research Credits, four capped permanent tracks and next rank cost; purchases are optional before immediate retry.
  4. Free blueprint achievements: new cards join later random draws automatically. Keep prerequisites, module caps and exact per-pick descriptions.
  5. Atomic account/reward checkpoint settlement and recovery. Run upgrades reset; credits, Hangar ranks, unlocks and records persist.
  6. Local revive/+50% credit placement test doubles, then repeated human/device tests. Defer live ads, billing and additional progression systems until the loop is measured.

Persist credits, Hangar ranks, unlocks, records and cosmetic ownership; temporary abilities reset. Use a bounded pending/latest-settled reward receipt and atomic save replacement. The existing proposal checkpoints earned rewards every ten active seconds and on backgrounding; terminated-process recovery settles to results, while full active-run resume is deferred. These remain native implementation requirements. Offline tests prove in-memory transaction behavior only.
