Human Resources — infantry/weapons production authority and future integration data

Current owner instruction supersedes the earlier gripping/seated-pose proposal:
ALL CHARACTER MODELS ARE UNARMED TRUE T-POSE, INCLUDING THE BODY INTENDED FOR
JEEP CREW. Rigging, equipping weapons, seating and armed assemblies come LATER.
No rigging, posing, animation, seating or armed assembly is part of this pass.

Current production scope: eight independent models
1. infantry-rifleman-body: compact navy helmet, shorts, coral sleeve accents;
   1.6 m height target, unarmed true T-pose, empty palms.
2. infantry-shotgunner-body: short broad silhouette, wide helmet, padded vest,
   bare forearms; 1.5 m height target, unarmed true T-pose, empty palms.
3. infantry-rocketeer-body: tall lanky silhouette, angular ear-pad helmet,
   long trousers and small integral backpack; 1.8 m height target, unarmed true
   T-pose, empty palms.
4. weapon-carbine: separate single-barrel carbine, wood stock, square magazine,
   navy/coral paint; approximately 0.70 m long, no hands/body fragments.
5. weapon-shotgun: separate chunky break-action shotgun with EXACTLY TWO
   side-by-side barrels, wood stock/foregrip, navy/coral paint;
   approximately 0.70 m long, no hands/body fragments or loose shells.
6. weapon-rocket-launcher: separate shoulder tube with two grips and the
   explicitly approved INTEGRAL CORAL ROCKET NOSE; approximately 1.15 m long,
   no human fragments or detached ammunition. Its loaded visual state is
   intentional, not a geometry error or a separate projectile/animation.
7. jeep-minigun-head: independent six-barrel navy/coral gun head, compact
   integral ammo box and trunnion; no pedestal, operator or vehicle fused in.
8. jeep-minigun-mount: independent baseplate, short column and fork bearing;
   no gun, vehicle, floor or crew fused in.
The rifleman T-pose body will also supply future jeep crew. There is NO separate
seated character model to generate, and no standing body is placed through a
seat to imply a completed seated fit.

Visual direction
- Existing low-poly navy/coral military family with broad hand-painted gouache
  surfaces and warm comic faces. Read major shapes at elevated mobile scale.
- Rifleman is the medium compact baseline; shotgunner is broader and shorter;
  rocketeer is taller and narrower. Their bodies must remain distinguishable
  before weapons are equipped.
- Future weapon silhouettes: slender/long single barrel, short/broad double
  barrel, and oversized loaded shoulder tube. These are visual role proposals,
  not combat rules, damage/range/AI/targeting or progression decisions.
- Preserve exaggerated heads, boots and simple mitten-like hands. No realistic
  camouflage noise, engravings, tiny accessories, text, labels or logos.
- Character targets 5,000–6,500 triangles; weapon/accessory targets 1,000–2,800.
  Report actual counts per component. These are art targets, not demonstrated
  mobile runtime budgets.

Current reference and model QA
- Body reference authority is its latest approved isolated UNARMED TRUE T-POSE
  image: both arms straight/horizontal, legs complete, empty hands and no gun
  fragments. Earlier armed concept sheets are not pose authorities.
- Weapon references contain one separate weapon only. No hands, person,
  mounting stand, additional view or discharge effect. Preserve the shotgun's
  two muzzle holes and the launcher's approved coral loaded nose.
- Normalize uniformly to metres, +Y up, muzzle/nose +Z. Retain raw downloads.
  No nonuniform stretching, rigging, animation or separate retexturing step.
- Measure actual generated geometry to record primary grip, support grip,
  shoulder rest and muzzle/fire-alignment data. These sockets describe the
  WEAPON, not a current body pose or a completed hand contact.
- Prefer the measured primary-grip origin where unambiguous. A centre/rear
  origin with measured external socket positions is acceptable while exact
  future equipment transforms remain unimplemented. Never invent coordinates
  by copying a guessed standing hand position.
- For the loaded launcher record: loaded_state=true; coral nose is integrated
  into this static mesh. Record forward axis/fire alignment for a FUTURE
  separate projectile. Do not extract, animate or fire that nose now.
- Inspect actual textured geometry from several angles for coherent stocks,
  grips, exact shotgun barrel count, tube/nose orientation and absent human
  fragments. Structural/export success alone is not visual acceptance.
- Actual weapon sockets belong in assets/production/weapon-sockets.json and
  are limited to the measured weapon IDs. No body grip-pose datums are imposed.
