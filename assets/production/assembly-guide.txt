Human Resources — static model assembly recipes

Authority: assets/production/assemblies.json
Reproduce final proof with healthy bundled Python:
  python tools/render_assemblies.py --edition final
The tool reuses the existing authoring-only numpy/Pillow environment and the
actual normalized GLB triangle/UV/base-colour data. It does not render through
or change the native runtime renderer.

Conventions
- Metres, right-handed +Y up, +Z forward, +X right.
- Load each node's normalized model_file, including its existing normalizing
  root. Do not apply its import scale twice. Put that imported hierarchy inside
  an external placement wrapper. Socket frames belong to the wrapper in
  normalized metres, never to a GLB internal raw-space/normalizing node.
- Traverse nodes in listed order. Unattached nodes use translation_m and yaw.
  Attached nodes use parent world transform multiplied by the named socket's
  local translation and +Y yaw. No reflections or nonuniform scale are used.
- source_sha256 records exactly which normalized models were reviewed.

Farmer and resident
- Straw hat now has a 1.05 m broad brim; cap width is 0.68 m. Their raw files and
  images are preserved. Uniform normalization replaced the guessed smaller
  dimensions rather than applying nonuniform attachment scale.
- Hats sit over the existing hair; concealed hair/cap overlap is intentional.
  These are separate accessories with no body mesh changes or animation.
- The straw socket's 180 degree yaw puts its low brim toward the rear, leaving
  the face clear from the front and the principal three-quarter angle.

Jeep
- Chassis translation [0,0.12,0]; wheel radius 0.30 m.
- Wheel socket positions are relative to the chassis. All axle centres become
  world Y=0.30 m, so tyres meet Y=0.
- The model's decorated hub faces local +X. Right instances have zero yaw;
  left instances yaw 180 degrees, pointing hubs to assembly -X. Roll axes
  follow the transformed local +X axis; no negative scale is needed.
- Existing standing soldier is not used as seated crew. A standing model
  clipped through a seat would not constitute an approved driver fit.

Tank
- Turret socket is at [-0.04,0.955,-0.24] relative to the hull. A shallow 0.04 m
  seating overlap joins its base to the hull ring.
- Its primary axis is +Y, with +Z cannon aim at zero yaw. The existing base
  footprint centred pivot remains intact. No turret/hull resizing is applied.
