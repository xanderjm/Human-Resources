MODULAR UFO — AUTHORING & ASSEMBLY

Based on the existing UFO body, beam emitter, laser mount and shield references.
Base size: 2.6 m diameter, 1.398 m tall. Metres, +Y up, +Z forward.
The ring is wider than v1 (2.2 m); the dome and fixed hull retain their size.
The existing generated models are preserved in their original folders.

RING ROTATION
Find the node named Outer_Ring_Pivot. Its rest position is [0, 0.35, 0].
Rotate its local Y axis. The ring shell, its painted surface, bezels and lenses follow it.
Keep Fixed_Hull stationary. Do not rotate the complete UFO root.
An optional Outer_Ring_Spin clip completes one revolution in 8 seconds.
Loop that clip in the engine, or drive the pivot directly.
The ring inner vertex radius is 0.604 m; the fixed bearing radius is 0.587 m.
The nominal radial gap is 17 mm. Even at the middle of an inner polygon edge,
at least 15.7 mm remains through a full revolution, including fitted upgrades.

Module GLBs have their attachment pivot at their root [0,0,0].
The complete ring GLB is centred on its own rotor pivot at [0,0,0].

EDITING
The source is mostly quads with triangle fans only at closed poles.
Raised panel and chip meshes have been removed. Panel shapes, thin joins, edge
wear and short ochre/orange brush accents are painted into the ring's UV map.
The ring and dome were captured directly from the clean model with orthographic
cameras, then painted with built-in ImageGen using the approved UFO/style sheet.
The full top view supplied the six lamp positions. The resulting maps are bound
to matching planar UVs, not applied as repeating generic material swatches.

Ring UV: u = 0.5 + x / 2.652; v = 0.5 + z / 2.652.
Dome UV: u = 0.5 + x / 1.0302; v = 0.5 + z / 1.0302.
Both screenshots and maps are 1254px square, looking down +Y with -Z at the top.
The outer vertex radius is 49.02% of image width. The ring bore is 22.78%.
Paint extends slightly beyond the footprints for texture filtering bleed.
No dome, lamps or antenna are baked into the ring map; they remain separate parts.
The dome has its own cobalt paint and broad pale-blue painted reflection.
Mint lenses have restrained emission. Faceted normals preserve the game style.
PNG masters are unmodified ImageGen outputs (1254 px square). Runtime GLBs embed
JPEG encodings at the same native size to reduce download size; no external
textures are needed. Material definitions are saved in ufo-source.json.
Runtime GLBs triangulate those surfaces and split vertices at flat normals/UV seams.
Each source surface is closed, consistently wound and non-degenerate.
OBJ keeps named parts and quads but cannot preserve parent-child hierarchy/pivots.
Use the GLBs or JSON for the hierarchy.

To rebuild with Python + NumPy + Pillow and the saved paint maps:
  python3 tools/build_ufo_modular.py

To create a native quad Blender scene when Blender is installed:
  blender --background --python tools/import_ufo_modular_blender.py
  blender --background --python tools/import_ufo_modular_blender.py -- --upgraded
This importer creates a new scene, packs the textures, preserves quads, parents the
parts, converts +Y-up to Blender's +Z-up and adds a ring animation.
Blender is unavailable on this machine, so this optional importer has not been
executed here. The delivered GLBs/OBJ/JSON have been validated directly.

PREVIEW
Serve the repository root and open /tools/model-viewer/modular.html.
Use Spin ring, Rotation, Explode parts, Wireframe and Configuration.
For new registered painting captures use ?template=ring, ?template=dome or
?template=full at a square viewport; use a full-page screenshot to capture all pixels.
Full-turn clearance concerns the authored geometry; no engine physics or gameplay
system is included. Upgrade tiers can replace modules under the same sockets.
