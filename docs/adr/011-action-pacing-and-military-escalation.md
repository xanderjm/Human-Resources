# Faster harvesting and escalating military population

Date: 26 October 2026. Status: owner-requested native playtest candidate.

The owner rejects the earlier modest ramp as too slow and asks for large-scale
abductions and much more military activity. The runtime spatial contract owns
this stronger pass. Abstract balance assumptions and simulator implementations
remain unchanged; they do not model physical placement or this encounter flow.

Travel starts at 3 m/s, gains 2 m/s over 45 active seconds, 0.05 m/s per
biological capture and 0.22 m/s per earned level after one, capped at 6.8 m/s.
Pause, background and drafts freeze the ramp. Saved active time, captures and
level reconstruct it. Overdrive still improves harvesting/firing separately.

Native lifts use 1.2 seconds per weighted load instead of 1.6. The generated
base capture value comes from `abduction.base_seconds_per_load`; the older
catalogue baseline remains an abstract study input. Species loads, committed
beams, one starting beam, random Splitter eligibility, six hardware slots,
research/level bonuses, sweep participation, two-slot vehicles, Winch gates and
the two-second tank floor retain their owners. The existing biological floor
still limits strong builds. Faster lifts increase rewards and account earnings;
prices and credit conversion are unchanged and need human economy review.

Civilian delivery doubles from 1.4 to 2.8 bodies/s, still multiplied by actual
travel relative to starting speed. Its cap increases from 42 to 64. Mixed later
pockets favour people, while the small-animal guarantee remains. Delivery stays
atomic in quartets, with measured footprints and at most one blocked pocket
retained. No population backlog or extra dependency is introduced.

rockets48s, tank65s and boss100s. Successful introductions retain their bits and
wait for legal complete placement. One military formation is allowed before18s,

Game codec6/account2 stay. Authority17 and the generated numerical fingerprint

Existing owner apps and saves are preserved. Host tests, scripted comparisons
