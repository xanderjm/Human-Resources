# Human Resources Product Brief

Product kind: game. Status: native playable implementation and qualification in progress, 25 October 2026. This brief collects the existing project direction and dated owner decisions. Numerical tuning remains a playable candidate; final release approval has not been recorded.

## Identity and purpose

- Name: Human Resources; namespace/target prefix: `human_resources`.
- App ID: `com.xanderjm.humanresources`, following Midnight Menu's developer namespace with a distinct identity.
- Build profiles: macos, windows, linux, ios, android. iPhone is the first intended mobile launch; macOS is the local development host. Other profiles await qualification.
- Optional foundation features: none. Networking, deterministic replay authority and text middleware are not selected by setup alone.
- Experience: overhead UFO abduction and survival. Fly a modular saucer, collect humans and animals with tractor beams, fight military resistance, and choose upgrades during a run.
- Current deliverable: a self-contained native playable built on the development foundation, preserving existing art, models, fonts and balance work. Portable gameplay, progression and protected saves now exist, with a native 3D client under integration. Runtime animation, portrait presentation, feedback and mobile qualification are actively being verified. Successful headless tests do not establish final art, fun or release readiness.
- First playable: farmland endless runner, saucer movement, biological captures, automatic lasers, enemy pressure, upgrade choices, death and restart. The next native milestone now integrates the existing Suburbia and Desert kits with session location selection; Suburbia uses the animated resident/nightcap. Shared gameplay rules and account progression remain unchanged. Three-theme source/build tests pass; current visual and mobile refresh remain separate qualification.
- Intended audience: teens and adults (13+), confirmed by the owner on 25 October 2026. Complete the platform content-rating questionnaires before submission; this audience choice does not assign a store rating. Review any future advertising/consent integration against this audience.

## Experience and interaction

- Proposed flow: title/start, run with compact top HUD, paused upgrade choice, pause/settings, death/revive decision, results with optional Hangar purchases and immediate restart. All unlocked blueprints enter later random draws automatically; there is no mandatory blueprint preparation screen. Ordinary play keeps the bottom of the scene open.
- Touch movement is the mobile priority. The native candidate uses a floating drag stick, with keyboard and controller movement for local development. Semantic actions precede gameplay; final sensitivity, thumb reach and device cancellation still need portrait playtests.
- The current engagement candidate gives movement an immediate purpose: fly close to accelerate captures, qualify three close sweeps within five active seconds for six seconds of Overdrive, and gain 50% capture/fire cadence during that boost. Captures during the burst retain rewards but do not refresh/precharge it; three fresh sweeps rearm it after expiry. Qualification requires meaningful moving participation in each capture. Ordinary automatic capture/fire remains available. Starter Dash moves 2m with 0.15s protection and a four-second cooldown, triggering an immediate ordinary counter-volley when a valid target exists; Phase Drive improves distance/protection/cooldown. All timers freeze with gameplay. These are playable tuning candidates under native review, not accepted human-fun results.
- Portrait is the primary mobile orientation, explicitly selected by the owner on 25 October 2026. Update the project contract, both mobile shells, gameplay camera, controls and UI together; landscape is no longer the primary product assumption. Review small portrait phones and device safe areas using actual native captures.
- Owner playtest direction, 25 October: show world characters/objects at about three-quarters their previous size, while the player ship keeps its previous apparent size. The player rig, mounted gear and intake must stay coherent. Expand useful vertical flight, make civilians run away below scroll speed, accelerate encounters and let scroll speed grow with capture/power. Improve enemy intentions, explicit damage feedback and the animated level-up flow. Authority 7 retains the varied social groups, local fleeing and measured compact upgrade UI, adding body-aware military approach/ingress to prevent shared flank destinations collapsing enemies into one silhouette. Boss and recently engaged damaged-vehicle vitality show actual combat progress. The separately launched Current Preview remains authority 6 and restores the original apparent ship size; human balance/feel remains under iteration.
- Respect safe areas and pause on background/focus loss. Cancel stale captured input and reset elapsed frame time on return. Background/ad time must not advance simulation or placement clocks.
- Damage feedback identifies actual successful collision or projectile hits while retaining shield/hull meaning and generic unknown/cold-load fallback. Provenance is transient presentation data, not inferred from nearby actors or persisted as a death cause. A shared portrait-stage policy now keeps full flight visible on tablets/wide windows while ordinary accepted phone pixels remain exact; see `docs/adr/004-shared-flight-framing.md`. Enlarged RUN surfaces now use bounded header/action strips with consumed-glyph and full-rig checks at up to3.2x text, preserving the ordinary phone view. A separate short-window rail-budget issue at3.2x remains under correction; current-device reachability is not established.
- Use readable text, adequate touch targets, labelled controls and information beyond colour alone. Reduced motion and unavailable audio/haptics must preserve playability. Native screen-reader bridges exist; gameplay accessibility needs product design and device testing.
- Initial content language is English. Review localisation, text expansion and font coverage before adding languages.
- Missing or corrupt required cooked assets fail startup with actionable diagnostics. Distinguish a saving failure from a new save. Loading, retry, cancellation and recovery UI belong with the actual feature that can fail.
